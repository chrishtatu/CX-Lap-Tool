Several files from the same race and course (different riders) can be compared side by side.

Everything runs inside this page: your file is not uploaded anywhere, and it works offline.
The race start and finish are found from your power (standing still, then a big jump; the last big effort before you stop pedaling), and the lap line is where you started. You can change all three in Settings.

Course map

Arrows show riding direction. Pinch to zoom (or use + and −); once zoomed in, drag with one finger to move around. At full view a one-finger swipe scrolls the page.

Section times by lap

Cell color = time vs. your median for that section (blue = faster, red = slower). SD = lap-to-lap standard deviation; smaller is more consistent.

Time gap along the lap

The lap you pick is drawn as the flat dashed zero line; every other lap shows how far behind (up) or ahead (down) of it you were at each point. Where a line climbs steeply, that's where you lost time to it. Drag across any chart to zoom; double-click to reset.

Corners, lap by lap
